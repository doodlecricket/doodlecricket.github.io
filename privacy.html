Privacy Policy

Doodle Cricket and the games on doodlecricket.github.io are free to play. This page explains how information is collected, used and disclosed when you use this website.

Information collection and use

We do not ask you to create an account or provide personal information. Game progress and settings are stored on your own device.

Third-party services

This website uses third-party services that may collect information, including cookies and device identifiers, to show and measure ads and to understand how the site is used:

  • Google AdSense and Google Ad Manager (advertising)
  • Setupad (advertising and the cookie consent banner)
  • Plausible Analytics (privacy-friendly, cookie-free visitor statistics)
  • Additional advertising and video-ad partners whose scripts load from cutleryneighbouringpurpose.com and 360playvid.info

Where required, you can accept or refuse advertising cookies using the consent banner shown on the site.

Cookies

Cookies are small files used as anonymous identifiers. This site does not set cookies itself, but the advertising providers above may. If you refuse these cookies, you may see less relevant ads.

Links to other sites

This site links to other websites and games that are not operated by us. We have no control over, and assume no responsibility for, the content or privacy practices of those sites.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page.
