Google Snake Game

The classic snake game from Google Search: guide your snake around the field, eat apples to grow longer, and don't run into the walls or your own tail.

How to play

  • Steer with the arrow keys on a keyboard, or swipe on a phone.
  • Each apple makes the snake longer and adds to your score.
  • The longer you get, the less room you have, so plan your turns.
