Doodle Champion Island Games

Overview

Released on July 23, 2021, the Doodle Champion Island Games is an interactive Google Doodle created in partnership with Tokyo-based animation studio STUDIO 4°C. This mini-game series celebrates Japanese culture and the spirit of the Olympics.

Gameplay

  • Players control Lucky, a calico cat athlete, exploring Doodle Champion Island.
  • The game features seven sport mini-games and various side quests.
  • Players can join one of four color teams to contribute to a real-time global leaderboard.
  • The goal is to defeat each sport Champion and collect seven sacred scrolls.

Sports and Champions

  1. Table Tennis - Tengu
  2. Skateboarding - Tanuki
  3. Archery - Yoichi
  4. Rugby - The Oni
  5. Artistic Swimming - Princess Otohime
  6. Climbing - Fukuro
  7. Marathon - The Kijimuna

Team Mascots

  • Blue Team: Ushi
  • Red Team: Karasu
  • Yellow Team: Inari
  • Green Team: Kappa

Cultural Elements

  • The game incorporates various Japanese folktales and mythological creatures.
  • Locations span from Hokkaido to Okinawa, showcasing Japan's diverse natural beauty.
  • Characters like Kappa, Yatagarasu, and lion dancers represent different aspects of Japanese culture.

Development Insights

  • STUDIO 4°C adapted characters from well-known Japanese stories and folklore.
  • The studio aimed to blend traditional elements with the sport themes creatively.
  • The developers hope players enjoy the game while appreciating the underlying message of individual effort contributing to collective happiness.

Fun Fact

The developers expressed a hope for a "sanshoku dango" (three-colored dumplings) trend to emerge from the game's popularity.
