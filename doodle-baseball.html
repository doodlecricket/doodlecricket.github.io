Doodle Baseball

Doodle Baseball is the game Google made for the Fourth of July in 2019: classic cookout foods take on a team of peanuts. Play it here any time, free, on desktop or mobile.

Step into a baseball diamond where your favorite ballpark snacks come to life! Swing as a mighty hot dog, a powerful popcorn kernel, a cool ice cream cone, or a juicy watermelon slice.

How to Play

Simply click your mouse or tap your screen to swing when the peanut pitcher throws the ball. Time your hits perfectly to score runs!

Your Team

  • Hot Dog: Your classic cleanup hitter
  • Popcorn: Quick and poppy at the plate
  • Ice Cream: Cool under pressure
  • Watermelon: The heavy hitter
