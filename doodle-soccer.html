Doodle Soccer

Doodle Soccer is the goalkeeper game Google put on its homepage for the London 2012 Olympics. Stand in goal, face penalty after penalty, and see how many you can save.

How to play

  • Move the mouse, or drag your finger, to shift the keeper left and right.
  • Click or tap to dive for high and wide shots.
  • Keep saving: let too many past you and your run ends.

Outside the US it's called Doodle Football. More sports: Doodle Basketball, Doodle Baseball and Doodle Cricket.
