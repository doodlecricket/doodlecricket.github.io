Sports doodle games

Six of the games are sports, and most of them came out for a big tournament. Apart from Champion Island they are the quickest to learn: one key or one tap does nearly everything. For more bat-and-ball games, see all cricket games.

  • Doodle Cricket: a cricket (the insect) bats against snail bowlers. Time each swing and keep your wicket.
  • Doodle Baseball: snack foods bat against peanut pitchers. Long hits are measured in feet.
  • Doodle Football and Doodle Soccer: one penalty game with two names. You are the goalkeeper and dive left or right.
  • Doodle Basketball: sink as many baskets as you can before the clock runs out.
  • Champion Island: the biggest of them all, a whole island of Olympic sports mini-games and side quests.

Arcade doodle games

The arcade group mixes real doodles with Google games from Search and one mobile classic.

  • PAC-MAN: the first doodle you could play, with the original arcade game logic, graphics and sounds.
  • Google Snake: eat apples, grow longer, stay off your own tail.
  • Doodle Halloween: Momo the cat draws the symbol over each ghost to pop it.
  • Garden Gnomes: fling gnomes from a catapult and plant flowers where they land.
  • Bubble Tea: make each drink the way the customer ordered it.
  • Doodle Jump: bounce up an endless column of ledges. It is a mobile game, not a Google doodle.

Puzzle and card games

For something slower, try Google Solitaire, the card game from Search, or merge tiles in 2048 Cricket and 2048 Baseball. The original 2048 and 2048 Cupcakes are here too.

Doodle games you can't play here

Google has made many more doodle games than we host. These are not playable on this site; the links go to Google's own Doodle archive.

  • Coding for Carrots (4 Dec 2017): program a furry friend through 6 levels to reach its favourite food, for 50 years of coding languages for kids.
  • The Great Ghoul Duel (30 Oct 2018): the first multiplayer doodle, two teams of ghosts racing to collect spirit flames.

Frequently asked questions

What are Google Doodle games?

A Google Doodle is a short-lived version of the Google logo for a holiday, an anniversary or an event. A few of them are small games, like the 2017 cricket doodle or the 2019 Fourth of July baseball doodle.

Are these Google games free to play?

Yes. Every game here is free to play in your browser.

What is the most popular Google Doodle game?

On this site, Doodle Cricket gets the most visits, followed by Doodle Football.
